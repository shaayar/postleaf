# PostLeaf

> **A personal library of letters that grow with you, and a place where your words can comfort someone you've never met.**

## Version

v1.0

## Status

Draft

---

## 1. Vision

PostLeaf is a digital home for meaningful letters.

Instead of writing messages that disappear until a scheduled date, users build a lifelong collection of letters they can revisit, continue, share, or preserve.

Alongside their personal library, users can anonymously publish letters written for strangers going through similar moments in life, creating a quiet community built around empathy instead of social engagement.

---

## 2. Mission

Help people preserve their thoughts, emotions, memories, and encouragement in the form of letters that remain part of their story instead of disappearing after delivery.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Most digital communication is temporary.

Messages disappear into chats.

Emails become clutter.

Future-letter apps hide letters until a specific date.

Personal journals remain private forever.

None of these capture the feeling of building a lifetime collection of meaningful letters while also allowing those experiences to help others.

People often write not because they already know who needs their words, but because they believe someone eventually will.

---

## 4. Product Philosophy

Every letter deserves to exist beyond the moment it was written.

A letter should never disappear simply because it has been delivered.

Writing is part of the author's journey as much as reading is part of the recipient's.

The library matters more than the delivery.

---

## 5. Core Principles

### Letters are permanent

Writing creates an artifact.

Delivery changes its state, not its existence.

---

### People grow through revisiting

Letters can be:

* continued
* reflected upon
* edited
* archived
* shared

---

### Stories are more valuable than metrics

The product measures memories instead of engagement.

Avoid:

* follower counts
* likes
* streaks
* leaderboards

Prefer:

* chapters
* milestones
* collections
* reflections

---

### Community through empathy

Letters are shared because they may help someone else.

Not because they perform well.

## Product North Star

> **Build a place where letters are not sent away and forgotten, but collected, revisited, and shared with intention. Every letter becomes part of someone's story, whether it's the writer's or a stranger's.**

This positioning gives PostLeaf a clear identity. It is no longer competing with "future email" services. It sits at the intersection of **personal journaling**, **letter writing**, and **anonymous acts of empathy**, with the library, not the inbox, at its center.
